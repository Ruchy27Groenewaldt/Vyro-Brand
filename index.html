Brand identity

The mark.

Three sides of one brand: the minimal Y monogram, the sprayed graffiti tag and the sharp high-fashion V. Built to live on garments, labels, packaging and screens.

Brand profile

Clothing is a canvas.

VYRO is a Namibian streetwear brand that blends graffiti culture, spray-paint artistry and contemporary fashion into limited-edition wearable art.

We don't mass-produce. Every VYRO garment is individually crafted with custom stencils, spray techniques and artistic detailing. Each collection is released as a limited drop, so every piece is exclusive and collectible.

Our style: hand-sprayed artwork, graffiti-inspired design, custom stencil graphics, oversized silhouettes and urban aesthetics.

Vision

To become Namibia's leading streetwear brand, recognised across Africa and beyond for turning fashion into artistic expression and inspiring creativity, confidence and individuality.

Mission

To create high-quality, handcrafted streetwear that empowers people to express who they are through bold design, authentic craftsmanship and limited-edition collections.

Design philosophy

Every collection tells a story.

Spray paint, custom stencils, layered textures and graffiti-inspired compositions turn garments into visual statements. This is how a VYRO piece is made.

  1. The story

    Each drop starts with a theme that shapes every stencil and colour.

  2. Cut the stencil

    Custom VYRO stencils are designed and cut for the collection.

  3. Spray by hand

    Artwork is sprayed and layered by hand, piece by piece.

  4. Finish & number

    Hand-finished, marked with its collection and numbered as a limited edition.
